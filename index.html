Welcome on my webpage!

Hello, I am a part-time postdoctoral researcher at RICAM Institute in Linz, Austria, under the supervision of Argyrios Petras. I completed my PhD in Bordeaux in March 2026, under the supervision of Michael Leguèbe and Annabelle Collin.

I am interested in PFA mathematical and numerical modelling, which was the topic of my PhD and is the topic of my postdoc work. PFA, or Pulsed Field Ablation, is an ablation technique used in cardiac or tumoral ablations. It relies on electroporation, a phenomenon happening at cell scale: when submitted to high electric voltages, the cell membrane becomes permeable, which can lead to cell death.

I am also interested in homogenization.
